Chart trading

Set your stop and target by dragging them off your position.

Click the chart to place an order. Once you're in, drag a stop and a target straight from your position marker. When one fills, Datahedge cancels the other automatically.

  • One-cancels-other bracketsStop and target are linked, so you never end up with a stray order.
  • Every timeframeFrom 15-second bars to daily, with volume under every candle.
  • Fills on the chartSee exactly where you got in and out.

Order book & tape

Depth and flow that stay where you put them.

Scroll the order book or the time & sales tape and it stays put. No snapping back to the top while you're reading the market.

  • Traded volume at every priceIncludes trading from before you opened the market, not just what happened since.
  • Filter the tape by sizeHide the small prints and watch the size come in.
  • Streaming dataConnect your keys and the book and trades update the moment they change.

Security

Your keys never leave your computer.

Datahedge runs locally on your PC and talks to the exchange directly. There's no Datahedge server in between, and we never see your account, your keys or your trades.

  • Keys held in memory onlyThey're never written to disk. Close the app and they're gone.
  • Locked to your machineThe app's trading engine only answers to its own window, not to other programs or websites.
  • Streaming-only optionConnect a key just for fast data and keep paper trading.
